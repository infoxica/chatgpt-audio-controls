# ChatGPT Audio Controls 1.4.0

Arrange your Read Aloud controls around your workspace, or dock them directly above the ChatGPT message box.

- **Move and remember your player.** Drag the circular control to a corner, either side, or the composer dock. Magnetic previews show where it will land. Swap the seek bar and playback controls using their grips, or choose a layout in settings.
- **A more cohesive player.** Compact controls follow ChatGPT's appearance. Docking preserves the native input border and rounded corners, and left-side layouts follow the visible sidebar as it opens and closes.
- **Know when the full audio is ready.** An optional success notification offers Download as soon as the complete retained audio is available, even while playback continues.
- **Assign your own desktop shortcuts.** Fifteen actions cover playback, seeking, speed, volume, downloads, visibility, layouts, help and settings. Existing shortcut preferences are preserved.
- **Choose from 33 interface languages.** Sixteen languages join the previous 17, with right-to-left support for Arabic, Persian and Hebrew. ChatGPT continues to provide the audio language and voices.
- **More compact settings.** Open sections, Light/Dark/System icon buttons, clearer shortcut controls, and direct suggestion, bug-report and store-review links.
- **Responsive interfaces.** Narrow screens use composer docking and touch-friendly controls. Real Edge Android acceptance remains pending; responsive desktop tests do not establish Android support.

The extension and standalone userscript share the player and playback-recovery fixes. Browser popup, dashboard and assignable browser commands apply to the extension; the userscript includes its own local settings.

After updating, refresh your ChatGPT tabs and start a new session with ChatGPT's native **Read Aloud** button. Existing preferences are retained. **Reset layout** changes placement only; it does not reset audio preferences.

[Share feedback](https://chatgpt-audio-controls.infoxica.com/feedback?source=installed&version=1.4.0). Store review and availability are tracked separately for Chrome and Edge.
